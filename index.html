HOW TO READ THE LANDSCAPE

Geometry carries meaning.

The long colored ribbons are residual streams. Attention appears as a fan of query heads reading shared memory. Expert banks contain a mark for every available expert. Matrix sheets reveal their exact logical dimensions when selected.

Blocks retain their positions as you zoom. The folded arrangement makes the complete architecture fit on a laptop; distance in this map does not encode execution time or learned similarity.

Lines represent declared dependencies. No glowing expert is presented as active without a recorded input. Compressed memory reduces sequence positions, not feature dimensions. Auxiliary prediction modules remain separate from the main decoder chain.

The separate numerical microscope includes all weights and recorded intermediates of a tiny trained transformer. Those recordings are not DeepSeek activations.
